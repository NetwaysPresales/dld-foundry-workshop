DLD RENTAL SERVICES - CUSTOMER REPLY STANDARD
Document ID: RNT-PRC-004
Version: 2.0
Classification: Synthetic workshop procedure; not an official DLD policy

1. Required structure
A reply to a rent enquiry must: thank the customer, restate the question in one sentence, explain the applicable rule with its source, state what the customer should check or provide, offer a mediation information appointment when it applies, and close with the approved closing sentence.

2. Accuracy
State the maximum allowed increase only as a rule ("if your rent is 20% below the average, the maximum increase is 5%"). Do not calculate a final figure unless the customer gave both the current rent and the official average rental value. Do not guess the average rental value.

3. No legal advice
Do not promise the outcome of a dispute. Do not tell the customer to stop paying rent or to refuse to leave. Refer disputes to the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre.

4. Approved closing sentence
"This reply explains the general rules and is not legal advice. For a binding decision, the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre at the Dubai Land Department can help."

5. Tone and length
Plain, respectful English. At most 200 words.
