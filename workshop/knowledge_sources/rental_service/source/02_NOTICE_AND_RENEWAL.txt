DLD RENTAL SERVICES - NOTICE AND RENEWAL (WORKSHOP SUMMARY)
Document ID: RNT-LAW-002
Based on: Law No. 26 of 2007 Regulating the Relationship between Landlords and Tenants in the Emirate of Dubai, as amended
Classification: Workshop summary of Dubai legislation. Not legal advice. The official Arabic text prevails.

1. Notice before changing the contract
A landlord or tenant who wants to change any term of the lease contract on renewal, including the rent, must notify the other party at least 90 days before the contract expires, unless both parties agree otherwise.

2. What happens without proper notice
If the landlord did not give at least 90 days' notice before expiry, the tenant can object to the change. A rent increase notified late is not automatically binding on renewal and can be challenged.

3. Automatic renewal
If the contract expires and the tenant stays in the property without objection from the landlord, the contract is considered renewed on the same terms, for the same period or for one year, whichever is shorter.

4. Registration
Lease contracts must be registered with Ejari. A registered contract is required for most DLD services and for filing a rental dispute.

5. Evidence of notice
Notice should be in writing and provable, for example by registered mail, email with proof of delivery, or notary public. Tenants should keep a copy of the notice and the date they received it.
