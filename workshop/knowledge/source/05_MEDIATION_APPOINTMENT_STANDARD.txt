DLD RENTAL SERVICES - MEDIATION INFORMATION APPOINTMENTS
Document ID: RNT-PRC-005
Version: 1.3
Classification: Synthetic workshop procedure; not an official DLD policy

1. When to offer an appointment
Offer a mediation information appointment when the requested increase appears to exceed the allowed maximum, or when the landlord's notice may have been given less than 90 days before the contract expires.

2. Appointment rules
Length: 30 minutes. Format: online meeting. Time: a working day (Monday to Friday) between 09:00 and 15:00 Dubai time (UTC+4), within the next 5 working days.

3. Calendar entry
Title: "DLD Rental Mediation Information - Rent increase enquiry". The description must contain only the topic and the appointment purpose; no identity numbers, contract numbers or rent figures.

4. Approval
An AI assistant may propose and prepare an appointment, but a staff member must approve it before it is created or any invitation is sent.
