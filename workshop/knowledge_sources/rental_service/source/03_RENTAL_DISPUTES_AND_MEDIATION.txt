DLD RENTAL SERVICES - RENTAL DISPUTES AND MEDIATION (WORKSHOP SUMMARY)
Document ID: RNT-LAW-003
Based on: Decree No. 26 of 2013 Concerning the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre in the Emirate of Dubai
Classification: Workshop summary of Dubai legislation. Not legal advice. The official Arabic text prevails.

1. Who decides disputes
Rental disputes between landlords and tenants in Dubai are handled by the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre (RDC) at the Dubai Land Department.

2. Amicable settlement first
Disputes go through an amicable settlement (mediation) stage, where a mediator tries to help both parties agree before the case goes to a judge.

3. What a tenant needs to file
A registered (Ejari) lease contract, the tenant's identification, the landlord's notice or message about the increase, and any proof of payments. A filing fee applies; the current fee is published on the DLD website.

4. What DLD staff may do
DLD staff may explain the rules, point the tenant to the RDC and the Rental Index calculator, and offer a mediation information appointment. Staff must not predict the outcome of a dispute or tell a tenant to stop paying rent.
